Who Am I?

My name is Farouk Shoucair and I am a Computer Engineering student at the University of Texas at Dallas (UTD). I have a deep interest in creating meaningful projects that benefit myself and others. My ultimate goal is to specialize in the intersection of Artificial Intelligence and Robotics.

Why did I create this website?

I initially wanted to create this website for personal use. Ever since I started college, I have tracked the exact hours I spend studying and working. After doing this on pen and paper for a long time, I wanted to digitize the process. I looked for websites that could track my time the way I wanted, but couldn't find any that fit my needs. I realized that if I needed a tool like this, many others probably do too.

Price?

This website will ALWAYS remain free. Under no circumstances will there ever be a subscription model or advertisements.
